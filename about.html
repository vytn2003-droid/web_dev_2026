Quick summary

My name is Vy Nguyen, and I’m studying Information Communication Technology. I’m interested in how people use technology in real settings and how we can design systems that are clear, accessible, and secure.

Right now, I’m building a multi-page website using semantic HTML. The point of this version is not flashy features, but writing content that has a purpose and organizing it with headings and meaningful page sections.

Interests

Web structure and accessibility

I like the idea that a website should still “work” when it’s read by a screen reader or viewed without styling. Semantic HTML supports that by giving each part of the page a job and a name.

Security mindset

I’m also interested in cybersecurity topics like account protection, MFA, and safer online habits. Security often fails when interfaces are confusing, so clear structure and communication matter.

Plans after graduation

After I graduate, I plan to move to Dallas, Texas and pursue a career in cybersecurity sales engineering. I’m drawn to work that combines technical understanding with communication and problem-solving.

This website will grow over time into something closer to a personal portfolio, with links to class projects and short write-ups explaining what I built and what I learned.
